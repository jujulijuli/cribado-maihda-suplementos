Intersectional inequalities in cancer screening participation in Spain, 2009–2023

Interactive supplementary material for: Intersectional inequalities in cancer screening participation in Spain over six survey waves (2009–2023): a cross-classified MAIHDA analysis. J Epidemiol Community Health (submitted).

  • Supplementary Figure S1 – Interactive intersectional trajectories: deviation of each stratum from the average stratum across survey waves (probability scale).
  • Supplementary Figure S2 – Interactive version of Figure 3: deviations in predicted uptake from the average stratum (percentage points) for all strata, screenings and waves.

Data: Spanish national health surveys (EESE 2009, ENSE 2011, EESE 2014, ENSE 2017, EESE 2020, ESdE 2023; INE / Ministerio de Sanidad public-use microdata). Models: null cross-classified MAIHDA (stratum × autonomous community), lme4.
