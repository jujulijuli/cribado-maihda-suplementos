Territorial and intersectional inequalities in cancer screening participation as organized programmes expanded in Spain (2009–2025)

Supplementary material for: Territorial and intersectional inequalities in cancer screening participation as organized programmes expanded in Spain (2009–2025): a cross-classified MAIHDA analysis. J Epidemiol Community Health (submitted).

Librero-López J, Pérez-Vicente R, Cayuela A, Cayuela L, Redín-Sagrero P, Egozkue I, Etxegia J, Oscoz-Villanueva I, Tamayo I, Enguita-Germán M, Ibáñez-Beroiz B, Pastor-Valero M, Gimeno-Feliu L.

Interactive supplementary figures

  • Supplementary Figure S1 – Interactive intersectional trajectories: deviation of each stratum from the average stratum across survey waves (probability scale).
  • Supplementary Figure S2 – Interactive version of Figure 3: deviations in predicted uptake from the average stratum (percentage points) for all strata, screenings and waves.
  • Supplementary Figure S3 – Absolute participation gaps by component (2009–2025): education component, origin component, and total combined gap, with 95% confidence intervals derived by the delta method.

Supplementary methods and tables

  • Supplementary document – Supplementary Tables S0–S3 and Online Supplemental Methods (SM1–SM4): survey weight construction, parametric bootstrap for variance component CIs, gap decomposition and delta-method CIs, and Simple-Means MAIHDA.

Data: Spanish national health surveys (EESE 2009, ENSE 2011, EESE 2014, ENSE 2017, EESE 2020, ESdE 2023, ESdE 2025; INE / Ministerio de Sanidad public-use microdata). Models: null cross-classified MAIHDA (stratum × autonomous community), lme4, unweighted.
